    Design questions to discuss

    01 / Replacement pressure

    Five first-step fusions compete for four loadout slots. Should every branch have a clear role, or is the pleasure mainly collecting stronger spells? This prototype does not assign damage or prove balance.

    02 / Chain concentration

    Tempest Crown depends on Frostfire Bolt and Hailstorm, giving Fire, Ice and Wind a shared payoff. Earth has no second-step payoff yet. Is that asymmetry interesting or disappointing?

    03 / Discovery friction

    Twelve spells give 66 different unordered pairs, but only six recipes. With no result preview, how will players get useful clues? Hints, exact reveal rules and duplicate-forge behavior need decisions.
